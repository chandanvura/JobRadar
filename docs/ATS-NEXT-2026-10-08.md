## Engineering change

The existing Workday adapter gains `workday_all`: complete pagination plus fresh details for every advertised posting, including titles outside the portal's eligibility filter. BrowserStack and Remitly have no usable India facet, so their full global inventory is verified before normal eligibility rules run. Vanguard uses the exact India facet returned by its public API.

The adapter checks advertised totals, page sizes, unique listing paths, unique detail IDs, complete descriptions, consistent listing/detail titles and first-page snapshots before and after details. Moving inventories trigger bounded existing snapshot retries. Missing details remain limited; they cannot pass the release probe. No guessed tenants, retired boards, invented country facets or inferred location/date fields are introduced. All 272 verified details preserve source locations; all 272 absolute publication dates are unknown because the advertised posting labels are relative. Workday `startDate` is not assumed to mean publication date.

